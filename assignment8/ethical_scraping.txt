1. Which sections of the website are restricted for crawling?

Disallowed sections of the website include parts for editors and administrators
as well many notice boards, policies about abuse, and their blacklist. It's
really anything having to do with running the site.

2. Are there specific rules for certain user agents?

Yes. Certain agents aren't allowed all together, usually for ignoring robot.txt.

3. Reflect on why websites use robots.txt and write 2-3 sentences explaining
its purpose and how it promotes ethical scraping.

Many of the rules reflect on not over loading the system, which several of
the disallowed bots have done. The robot.txt keeps wikipedia functioning
so anyone can scrape the site and/or use the site.
